## Welcome to the AgStack Community!

We're glad you're here!

Please note that _**it is not required**_ that your company or organization be a [member of AgStack](/membership/) in order to participate in our projects.

All contributors are welcome here, as long as they…

* have a desire to improve agriculture through open technologies, and 
* agree to obey the [Linux Foundation Code of Conduct]({{ site.coc }}).

This last point is very important. We have a zero-tolerance policy for any sort of unwelcoming or abusive behavior by our contributors. Please read and become familiar with the [code of conduct]({{ site.coc }}) before participating in AgStack projects and communities.
